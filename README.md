# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview

This project analyzes sales performance data to identify trends in revenue, profit, customer segments, and regional performance.
The goal is to help business stakeholders understand what drives sales and where improvements are needed.
## Business Problem

The company wants to understand:
Which regions perform best
Which products generate the most revenue
How customer segments differ in purchasing behavior
Overall sales trends over time
## Tools Used
Power BI (Data visualization & dashboard creation)
Excel (Data storage & preparation)

## Key Features

* Sales trends over time (Order Date)
* Regional performance comparison
* Segment analysis (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
* Sub-category performance breakdown
* KPI cards (Sales, Profit, Quantity, Discount)

##  Key Insights
The West region generated the highest sales
The Consumer segment contributed the largest share of revenue
Phones and Chairs are the top-performing product categories
Certain regions show consistent underperformance and require attention
## Business Recommendations
Focus marketing strategies on high-performing regions and products
Investigate underperforming regions for potential improvements
Optimize inventory for top-selling products
Increase targeting of high-value customer segments
